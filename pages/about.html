About EcoHaven Furniture

Established in 2026 in Candaba, Pampanga, EcoHaven Furniture is a furniture venture focused on innovative, functional, and space-efficient designs.

The Meaning of Our Name

Our name combines "Eco," reflecting care for the environment and responsible sourcing, and "Haven," representing a safe, comfortable sanctuary. Together, these ideas guide our aim to create furniture that supports comfortable living while making better use of available space.

At EcoHaven Furniture, we believe useful furniture should be practical, durable, and attractive. We aim to respond to changing customer needs through thoughtful design, quality workmanship, and responsible use of materials.

Our Featured Innovation

Discover TetraTable

Our featured product, TetraTable, is a multifunctional furniture solution designed to make every space count. Its compact side-table design transforms into four separate chairs, giving homes and small spaces a flexible table-and-seating solution in one product.

By combining two furniture functions in one design, it helps users save space and enjoy greater flexibility in homes, apartments, condominiums, dormitories, and other compact urban settings.
