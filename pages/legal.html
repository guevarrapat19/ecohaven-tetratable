Business Identity: EcoHaven Furniture operates its main production facility in Dalan Batu, Pasig, Candaba, Pampanga, Philippines. Inquiries may be directed to +63 981 255 8028 or ecohavenfurniture@gmail.com.

Product Availability & Specifications: The TetraTable is a multifunctional wooden furniture piece designed to function as an accent side table and separate into four standalone chairs. Built using Philippine Mahogany wood with natural Barnis stain finish. All custom and handcrafted furniture commissions require a 50% non-refundable earnest deposit upon order placement to immediately secure and allocate certified Philippine Mahogany/Narra timber reserves, initiate specialized kiln moisture stabilization (11.4% MC), and fund master joinery labor at our Candaba, Pampanga workshop.

2. Philippine Data Privacy Notice (Republic Act No. 10173)

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), EcoHaven Furniture protects personal data collected during inquiries, transactions, and fulfillment.

Information Collected: Customer name, delivery coordinates within Pampanga and Central Luzon, contact telephone number, and payment transaction reference numbers.

Use of Information: Personal information is utilized strictly to process fabrication orders, schedule white-glove delivery, verify GCash payment references, and provide after-sales warranty care.

Protection Safeguards: Customer records and payment proof screenshots are secured in encrypted Firebase cloud storage with restricted administrative access. We never sell, rent, or distribute customer details to third parties.

3. 30-Day Return & Exchange Policy

30-Calendar-Day Request Period: Customers may report defects, missing components, or transit damage within thirty (30) days from delivery.

Delivery Inspection Guarantee: Customers are encouraged to inspect the TetraTable alongside our white-glove courier team. If structural defects or finish transit damage are present, free return or immediate replacement will be coordinated by our Candaba workshop.

Change-of-Mind Guidelines: Items returned for change-of-mind must be in original, unblemished condition with packaging intact.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the TetraTable separate into four chairs?

The TetraTable features precision interlocking mortise joinery. The four chairs slide together cleanly into a unified 45cm x 45cm x 50cm side table cube without requiring tools, hardware, or complex latches.

What is the official retail price?

The TetraTable 4-in-1 Suite is ₱6,934.00. You can also reserve your unit with a 50% Earnest Down Payment of ₱3,467.00, paying the balance upon delivery.

How is the wood protected against termites and humidity?

All Philippine Mahogany is kiln-dried to 11.4% moisture content and treated with deep-penetrating Solignum chemical barrier against termites and powder-post beetles.
